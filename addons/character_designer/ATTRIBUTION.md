# Third-party attribution

## Rain control-widget geometry

Character Designer includes normalized wire geometry derived from the control
widgets in the **Rain v3** character rig published by Blender Studio and
Demeter Dzadik.

- Requested credit: **Rain Rig (CC) Blender Foundation | studio.blender.org**
- Source: <https://studio.blender.org/characters/rain/v3/>
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Character Designer changes: the hand, pole-arrow, pole-line, foot-outline,
  heel-roll, master, finger-ring, and shoulder/clavicle-arch silhouettes were extracted or
  recreated as standalone vertex/edge wire geometry, normalized, procedurally
  scaled, reoriented, and mirrored for arbitrary humanoid rigs. Rain's objects,
  materials, scripts, drivers, and rig implementation are not bundled.
